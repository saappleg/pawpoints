Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Pet Care by Steven or log in using Google Sign-In, we collect basic profile details including your name, email address, profile picture (if provided), and phone number/address details you voluntarily add for pet sitting services.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Our application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate your identity and facilitate seamless account log-in. We only access basic profile information (openid, profile, email).

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, advertising networks, or external brokers under any circumstances.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account information and pet details are securely managed using Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore database infrastructure with encrypted data transmission.

4. Contact & Data Deletion

If you wish to request account deletion or have questions regarding your data privacy, please contact us at contact@petcarebysteven.me.
