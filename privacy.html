Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated August 11, 2026

This policy explains how Pet Care by Steven handles information when you browse the website, request care, create an account, use PawPoints, contribute to the trail guide, or enable notifications.

1. Information we collect

Account and contact details

Name, email address, authentication identifier, optional profile photo, phone number, service address, referral information, and account-verification status.

Pet and care information

Pet names, breeds, birthdays, photos, routines, medication notes, veterinarian details, emergency contacts, insurance information, and signed emergency authorization.

Home-access instructions

Information you choose to provide for service delivery, such as parking, entry, alarm, keypad, lockbox, mail, trash, plant-care, and household notes.

Portal activity

PawPoints balances, qualifying status, coupons, referrals, reviews, trail submissions, notification preferences, scheduled reminders, and administrative audit information.

Care-plan requests include the name, email, phone number, referral source, and care details entered in the request form. Service providers may also process IP address, browser, device, security-token, and request-log information needed to deliver and protect their services.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and maintain client profiles.
  • Respond to care requests, plan visits, follow care instructions, and contact clients about services.
  • Operate PawPoints, referrals, coupons, status levels, reviews, and the community trail guide.
  • Deliver optional personal updates, reminders, and administrative announcements.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

No sale or advertising use: Pet Care by Steven does not sell or rent personal information and does not use client profiles for third-party behavioral advertising.

3. Google sign-in and Firebase

Google sign-in requests only the basic openid, profile, and email scopes. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in credentials; Cloud Firestore stores portal records; and Firebase App Check helps detect unauthorized traffic. Pet Care by Steven does not receive your Google password.

Google user data is used only to provide and secure the requested account features. It is not transferred to data brokers, advertising networks, or unrelated third parties. Read Google’s Firebase privacy and security information.

4. Notifications and browser permissions

Push notifications are optional and require browser permission. If enabled, OneSignal may process a push-subscription identifier, an account-linked external identifier, device/browser information, notification status, and tags such as client role or PawPoints balance so the correct update can be delivered. Notification content may appear on a locked screen according to your device settings.

You can disable notifications in your browser or device settings at any time. Learn more from OneSignal’s personal-data documentation.

The portal also links to Walkies, WhatsApp, Google Maps, payment platforms, and other external sites. Information you provide after following an external link is governed by that service’s policy.

6. Browser storage, offline data, and analytics

The site uses local browser storage for preferences and limited feature state, including theme choice, install-prompt dismissal, and certain one-time bonus indicators. A service worker caches public website files for faster loading and offline access. Clearing site data in your browser removes these local items and the offline cache.

As of this policy date, Firebase Analytics is not loaded by this website. Infrastructure providers may still create operational, security, and delivery logs as described in their own policies.

7. Public contributions

Approved reviews, trail names, trail descriptions, locations, and selected gallery content may be displayed publicly. Do not submit private home-access information, personal contact information, or another person’s image in a public contribution without permission.

8. Retention, security, and your choices

Information is kept while an account is active and as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain transaction and safety records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards include HTTPS, Firebase access rules, App Check, restricted administrator access, and provider encryption; however, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

You may edit available profile fields in the portal, disable push notifications in your device settings, or request access, correction, or account deletion by emailing support@petcarebysteven.me. Some records may be retained when reasonably required for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or business-record purposes.

9. Children and policy changes

The portal is intended for clients who can arrange pet-care services and is not directed to children under 13. Pet Care by Steven does not knowingly create accounts for children under 13. Material policy updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
