Wooded trails

Mt. Airy Forest

Cincinnati Parks lists Mt. Airy as its largest park at 1,459 acres, with miles of hiking trails, paved walkways, and leashed dogs welcome. The enclosed Doris Day Dog Park is located at 2970 Westwood Northern Boulevard.

Best for: sniff-heavy enrichment and shaded terrain. Confirm current trail closures and dog-park maintenance before visiting.

Open mileage

Otto Armleder Memorial Park

Great Parks lists a 2.9-mile easy shared-use trail, a 1.2-mile connector toward Lunken, open green space, and a spacious dog park. The 10-acre off-leash area includes separate large- and small-dog sections.

Best for: steady paved walking and dogs that prefer open sightlines. Watch for bikes and other shared-use traffic.
Official Armleder information ↗
Mixed surfaces

Ault Park

Cincinnati Parks welcomes leashed dogs and lists paved walkways plus hiking trails with steps, bridges, and stream crossings. The varied surfaces make it easy to adjust a route to a dog’s energy and mobility.

Best for: flexible routes that combine a calmer paved walk with short natural-trail sections.

Cincinnati leash basics

Cincinnati Park Board rules require dogs to be held by a leash in city parks except inside a designated enclosed dog park. Dogs must be leashed while entering and exiting a dog park, remain under the handler’s control, and be removed at the first sign of aggression.

Before every walk

  • 🌡️Check pavement temperature, heat index, storms, ice, and air quality.
  • 🦮Inspect collar or harness fit, identification, leash, and waste bags.
  • 💧Bring water and shorten the route for heat, age, illness, or low stamina.
  • 🐾Give other dogs space; an on-leash greeting is not automatically safe or welcome.
  • 📍Confirm current hours, closures, and posted rules at the park entrance.
